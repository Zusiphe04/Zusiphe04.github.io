# 1. Agile Team Charter — Reflection

## Situation
At the start of the LocalPro project, the team needed to turn a South African business directory concept into a functional web platform. The platform required multiple integrated features: a customer directory search, a step-by-step quote request wizard, a business owner dashboard, lead management tools, and a freemium subscription model (R0 Basic vs R299/month Pro). Because the project involved diverse frontend and backend responsibilities, we needed a clear working agreement to define project scope, team roles, and quality expectations.

## Task
My responsibility as Developer and Portfolio Author was to contribute to establishing our team's working agreements, take ownership of assigned frontend UI components and business dashboard features, track progress transparently on visual taskboards, and ensure all completed work satisfied our agreed Definition of Done.

## Action
I participated in defining our team purpose, communication standards, and operational agreements. I ensured that all my technical assignments—such as building the LocalPro search header, the guided quote request modal, the business profile settings form, and the subscription billing view—were broken down into clear tasks. During daily stand-up entries, I reported progress on my assigned components, flagged technical blockers early (such as multi-step form state management), and verified that UI features functioned correctly before marking them as complete.

## Result
The team charter provided a shared point of reference that kept our development organized and aligned with project deadlines. It reduced uncertainty regarding feature priorities and established clear accountability for task delivery. Through this process, I learned that an Agile Team Charter is a vital operational tool that ensures every team member understands their responsibilities, maintains quality standards, and communicates effectively throughout the software development lifecycle.
