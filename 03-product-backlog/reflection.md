# 3. Product Backlog — Reflection

## Situation
The LocalPro platform required a structured backlog to manage features across directory search, category filtering, business profile configuration, and subscription tiers. Prioritizing these items was critical to ensure core marketplace features—like finding a pro or listing a business—were functional before adding advanced tools.

## Task
My responsibility was to organize and prioritize backlog items for both customer-facing and pro-facing modules. I needed to document completed backlog checklist items, connect technical setup to UI outputs, and ensure clear traceability from backlog items to directory search components and business detail forms.

## Action
I organized the product backlog into functional work packages: Directory Search & Categories, Business Profile Setup, and Rating & Review Systems. I prioritized essential search features (location inputs, category quick-links) and core profile editing forms first. I supported these backlog items with database query structures and UI screenshot evidence displaying filtered category views (such as Plumbers and Electricians) and business detail entry fields.

## Result
The organized product backlog provided visibility into completed work versus remaining platform enhancements. It allowed the team to deliver a functional MVP with search, business profiles, and quote workflows. Through this process, I learned how to break large system capabilities into manageable backlog items and maintain traceability between planned features and delivered user interfaces.
