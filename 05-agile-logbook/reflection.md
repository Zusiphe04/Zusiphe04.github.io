# 5. Agile Logbook — Reflection

## Situation
Over the course of the LocalPro project, development progressed through sequential planning, implementation, debugging, and testing phases. Managing technical challenges—such as state management across the multi-step quote wizard, form validation, and responsiveness—required transparent tracking of daily progress and technical blockers.

## Task
My responsibility was to maintain a consistent log of development activities, record weekly stand-up entries, track taskboard movements from To Do to In Progress and Completed, and document technical impediments honestly rather than hiding development bottlenecks.

## Action
I documented weekly stand-up entries covering completed tasks, upcoming goals, and technical blockers across all development weeks. I used taskboard walkthrough questions to assess work flow, identify integration bottlenecks early, and track resolution steps for bugs (such as input validation handling and layout adjustments for mobile screens). I recorded technical impediments openly in the logbook to maintain an accurate audit trail of project challenges.

## Result
The Agile logbook provides a complete chronological record of the development journey from initial concept mapping to final interface polishing. Openly documenting blockers enabled faster issue resolution and prevented recurring bugs. I learned that an Agile logbook is an essential reflective tool that promotes team accountability, captures lessons learned, and provides evidence of continuous problem-solving.
